GAME MOMENTS

Mike is our main character, he grows up in a harsh environment and slowly loses his mind.
Filled with hate, he's now seeking revenge. One day, an incurable illness strikes the town, and Mike goes into a coma.
Desperate to save him, his scientist father resorts to his last option.. “Project 13”.
