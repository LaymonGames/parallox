GAME MOMENTS

Mike is our main character, he grows up in a harsh environment and slowly loses his mind.
Filled with hate, he's now seeking revenge. One day, an incurable illness strikes the town, and Mike goes into a coma.
Desperate to save him, his scientist father resorts to his last option.. “Project 13”.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

▸

What type of game is Parallox?

Parallox is a pixel-style, 2D, action RPG indie game.
▸

When will the full game be released?

The game is under active development, the estimated release window is: 2028–2029.
▸

Is it single player or multiplayer?

Parallox is a single player game.
▸

Who is the creator of Parallox?

Laymon Games — solo developer handling code, sound, art, story, and design.
▸

What engine is the game being made in?

Demo: GameMaker. Full release: may migrate to another engine.
▸

What platforms will it be available on?

PC (Windows, Linux), Mobile (Android), and potentially consoles.
